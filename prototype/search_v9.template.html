What changed from v8. v8 sorted 46 rows correctly and still made the shopper scroll past Verda, Aurora, Amber and Gold of the same area to reach a different one. The sort was never the problem — the unit was. A wax type is a choice you make after picking the area, not a competing search result.
One data gap this exposes. 94 of the 1,296 catalogue services carry no price, 79 of them visible and 19 with real orders against them — mostly hub-level rows where the price sits on the child. v9 will not pre-select an unpriced variant, but the underlying rows need fixing in TBL_services.
The clustering key. Area is the sorted set of qualifier:part pairs in the name, so Full Arms + Full Legs + Underarms and Full Arms + Underarms + Full Legs are one row. Wax-type words and wrapper words are pulled out first, which is what makes the two names identical.

Booking volume is real. Counts come from the 629,017-line booking export: 548,893 completed lines across 1,403 catalogue services, after excluding 80,123 cancelled and refunded lines. Waxing alone accounts for 335,656 of them.
Two exclusions applied. Female Disposable Combo ships with every order, so its 129,603 bookings would otherwise make it the top result for almost any beauty query — removed from popularity entirely, along with 94 other consumables and customer-invisible services. Separately, threading is filed under a sub-category named “Threading & Face Wax”, so a search for waxing matches it on filing alone. Those land in band 4 rather than leading — Eyebrows Threading is the highest-volume service in the catalogue and would otherwise top every waxing search.
