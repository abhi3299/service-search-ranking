Service search ranking

A search engine for a home-services marketplace — 1,532 services across seven verticals, retrieved by meaning as well as by words, and ordered by a sort you can read line by line.

Synthetic data. Every service, price, treatment line, order count and shopper history in this demo is generated. No real customer or catalogue data is present.

How it got there

Version 10.1
Spelling correction — Damerau–Levenshtein with transposition, over a vocabulary derived from the catalogue itself.
Version 10
Two filter layers gated by the query rather than the selection, so refining never empties the screen.
Version 9
Variant clustering — one row per treatment area instead of nine near-identical rows.
Version 8
The baseline: hybrid lexical + vector retrieval, five relevance bands, and the seven-key cascading sort.
Try hair removal, facail, massage, bridal makeup or laser. Switch personas in the header to see the ranking change with order history.
